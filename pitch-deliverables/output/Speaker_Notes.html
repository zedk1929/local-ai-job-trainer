JUDGE Q&A PREPARATION (RAPID-FIRE ANSWERS)
================================================================================

Q1: "Is this actually fine-tuning model weights or just prompting?"
A1: "Today it is prompt and rule refinement with deterministic schema enforcement and reserved verification. That means zero GPU training cost, instant deployment, and no catastrophic forgetting. Our Q2 roadmap adds optional on-device LoRA adapter tuning for tasks where prompt steering reaches its ceiling."

Q2: "What hardware does the user need?"
A2: "Any modern laptop with 8GB RAM can run Qwen 2.5 Coder 3B or Llama 3.2 3B smoothly via Ollama. On an Apple M-series or modest Nvidia GPU, it runs at over 50 tokens per second. We also provide a simulated Preview Mode so judges can test the app without downloading any local models."

Q3: "How do you guarantee privacy if you use Gemini or Claude?"
A3: "Privacy is user-controlled: only a small development sample of 10 to 20 anonymized examples is sent to the parent AI during setup to generate the rules. Once approved, 100% of the actual customer data and recurring production inference runs fully offline on the user's computer. The reserved evaluation set and future batches never touch the cloud."

Q4: "Why would someone pay $29 instead of just writing prompts in ChatGPT?"
A4: "Three reasons: First, 84% of surveyed professionals lack the prompt engineering expertise to achieve high F1 scores. Second, our system includes an automated evaluation harness with reserved test sets so you can mathematically verify improvement. Third, ChatGPT cannot run offline on confidential medical, financial, or legal records."
