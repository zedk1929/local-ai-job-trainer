[0:40 - 1:30 | 50s] PART 2: THE TEACHER-WORKER ARCHITECTURE (Slide 3)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"That brings us to how Apprentice works. We call it the Teacher-Worker architecture.

Instead of fine-tuning expensive models from scratch, you bring a few dozen examples of your job. 
Step 1: A frontier cloud AI—like Gemini 2.5 Flash or Claude 3.5 Sonnet—acts as the master craftsman. It inspects your baseline model's mistakes and formulates precise coding rules.
Step 2: You, the human, review and edit those rules in our UI under atomic revision control.
Step 3: Once approved, a lightweight 3-billion parameter model—Qwen 2.5 Coder—executes locally on your laptop via Ollama.

You get frontier intelligence during setup, but 100% offline privacy and zero token bills during execution. Your data never leaves your computer."

[1:30 - 2:30 | 60s] PART 3: THE HIGH-STAKES EXAMPLE — "GLASS IN LATTE" (Slide 4)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Now let me show you why this matters with a concrete, high-stakes example from our campus dining feedback dataset.

A customer submitted this review: 'Found a piece of shattered glass in my iced latte. Almost swallowed it!'

Out of the box, the baseline local AI catastrophically failed. It categorized the feedback as drink quality, called the sentiment 'unclear', and set needs_review to FALSE! A life-threatening safety emergency was completely buried in the inbox.

During the coaching phase, our parent AI caught this mistake and synthesized an explicit rule:
'Any physical hazard or foreign object in food or drink must NEVER be marked unclear. Assign negative sentiment and flag needs_review=true immediately.'

You approve the rule. When Apprentice runs again, it catches the glass immediately, flags the emergency, and alerts management within seconds. That is the power of human-approved parent coaching."

JUDGE Q&A PREPARATION (RAPID-FIRE ANSWERS)
================================================================================

Q1: "Is this actually fine-tuning model weights or just prompting?"
A1: "Today it is prompt and rule refinement with deterministic schema enforcement and reserved verification. That means zero GPU training cost, instant deployment, and zero catastrophic forgetting. Our Q2 roadmap adds optional on-device LoRA adapter tuning for tasks that hit prompting ceilings."

Q2: "What hardware does the user need?"
A2: "Any modern laptop with 8GB RAM can run Qwen 2.5 Coder 3B or Llama 3.2 3B smoothly via Ollama. On an Apple M-series or modest GPU, it runs at over 50 tokens per second. We also provide a simulated Preview Mode so judges can explore the full app without downloading any local models."

Q3: "How do you guarantee privacy if you use Gemini or Claude?"
A3: "Privacy is user-controlled: only a small development sample of 10 to 20 anonymized examples is sent to the parent AI during setup to synthesize the rules. Once approved, 100% of the actual customer data and recurring production inference runs fully offline on the user's computer. The reserved evaluation set and future batches never touch the cloud."

Q4: "Why would someone pay $29 instead of just writing prompts in ChatGPT?"
A4: "Three reasons: First, 84% of surveyed Cal Poly students lack the prompt engineering expertise to achieve high F1 scores. Second, our system includes an automated evaluation harness with reserved test sets so you can mathematically verify improvement. Third, ChatGPT cannot run offline on confidential medical, financial, or student records."
